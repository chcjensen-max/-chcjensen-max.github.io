Accessing Full-Text Articles via Columbia University

Last updated August 7, 2025

Publications supported by U.S. federal agencies (e.g. NIH-funded work) are freely accessible to the public and can easily be found with a simple search, e.g. on PubMed. Publications supported by private organizations (e.g. individual university or industry sources) may require accessing journal content behind a paywall.

Columbia University students and employees (for example, CUIMC Department of Anesthesiology staff and trainees) can access full-text journal articles via the Columbia University Augustus C. Long Health Sciences Library as follows:

  1. Go to the Augustus C. Long Health Sciences Library webpage.
  2. Click “PubMed.”
  3. Log in using your Columbia University UNI and password.
This may be different from your NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital CWID and password, and may require multifactor authentication (i.e. DuoMobile). Your UNI applies to both Columbia University and CUIMC systems (e.g. CUIMC email), but your password may differ across systems.
  1. Search within PubMed for your specific article of interest.
  2. Once identified, click one of the options under “Full Text Links,” often the specific journal’s website or Columbia e-link. If using e-link, you will typically need to select “Get Article” on the next page.
